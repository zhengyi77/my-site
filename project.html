Six things I want to build.

Not a portfolio — a list of ideas, with the reason each one is worth a weekend. The status is honest: two are finished, the rest are not.

The project ideas

Done

Study Timer

A Pomodoro timer: 25 minutes of work, 5 minutes of rest, a count in the page title so you see it from another tab.

Learned: setInterval and clearing it properly.

In progress

Flashcard Trainer

Flip a card, mark it known or unknown, and see the unknown ones come back more often. A tiny spaced-repetition system.

Learned: arrays of objects, and that state is just data.

Planned

Campus Map

A simple map of the campus with clickable buildings. Click one and a panel explains what happens there.

Would learn: positioning and SVG in the page.

Planned

Recipe Scaler

Paste a recipe, choose four portions instead of two, and every amount changes. The tricky part is fractions.

Would learn: form input, parsing numbers, edge cases.

Idea

Weather Card

One card, one city, the current temperature. It would be my first page that talks to someone else's server.

Would learn: fetch, promises, error states.

Idea

This Site, Rebuilt

Take this learning website and rebuild it in Vue, with the quotes as a component and no page reloads at all.

Would learn: components and reactivity.

Two of six are finished. That is the honest number, and it is the one I will say out loud in the presentation.
